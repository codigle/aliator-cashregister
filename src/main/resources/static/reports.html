How metrics work

Revenue counts only successfully recorded simulated checkouts; open checks, cancelled orders and demo tickets do not count. Seating time runs from QR join to checkout. Kitchen prep time runs from PREPARING to READY. When no data exists, figures are 0/—, not invented.

All payments on this demo are simulated and not financial settlements.

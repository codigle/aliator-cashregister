Tables and QR codes

Every table has its own current QR code. Closing a bill rotates the code.

Security: Cashier checkout closes the guest session and rotates its QR. The new QR must be displayed for the next group. You must display or print the newly generated QR for the next party. A copied QR can still be used by someone else during the current session.
